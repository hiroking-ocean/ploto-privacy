1. Introduction

Ploto ("the Application") is a desktop project management application for Windows. It provides comprehensive views including Gantt charts, Kanban boards, Priority Matrices, Member Task Views, Notes, and Whiteboards, all engineered so that users retain full ownership and custody of their data.

This Privacy Policy outlines how data is handled. In short: Ploto does not covertly harvest your personal data or transmit your project content to external servers.

2. Information We Collect

Zero collection by the application itself.

Ploto does not collect, record, transmit, or sell personal data such as names, email addresses, IP addresses, location data, hardware identifiers, or task contents.

The Application contains no analytics SDKs (e.g., Google Analytics), no telemetry, no automated crash reporters, and no advertising or behavioral tracking libraries. You do not need to register an account or sign in to use Ploto.

*Note: For details on standard platform diagnostic data (such as crash logs and aggregate install metrics) collected automatically by Windows OS and the Microsoft Store, please refer to Section 4.1.

3. Data Storage & Management

All projects, tasks, Gantt schedules, Kanban boards, priority matrices, member assignments, notes, whiteboards, ToDos, user preferences, and custom templates (.ploto-pack) are stored locally on your device, or on a shared network location (such as an internal network drive) that you explicitly choose, formatted as a .ploto SQLite database file.

Your files never leave your environment unless you choose to move or transfer them yourself. The developer does not operate any servers that receive, store, inspect, or process your project data.

4. Network Use & External Services

Ploto is designed to operate completely offline without sending project data across the network. However, for certain platform functions and optional user-initiated actions, the following external services are involved:

4.1. Microsoft Store & Windows Platform Diagnostics

The Application is distributed, updated, and optionally licensed via the Microsoft Store. In connection with these operations, Windows OS and the Microsoft Store platform may automatically collect standard system diagnostics and usage metrics (via Windows Error Reporting and Store telemetry):

• Distribution events such as installations, updates, and uninstalls

• Crash logs, hang reports, and stack traces if the application terminates unexpectedly

• Aggregate metrics such as OS version, device architecture, and general geographic region

These diagnostics are collected and managed at the OS/platform level by Microsoft, not through any custom tracking code embedded in Ploto. The developer only has access to anonymized, aggregated statistics (e.g., total crash counts, active install volume) via Microsoft Partner Center to identify bugs and improve reliability. Your private project files, tasks, and notes are never transmitted to Microsoft or the developer. These practices are governed by Microsoft's Privacy Statement.

4.2. Paid Licenses (Pro) & Payments (Stripe)

When purchasing a Pro license through the official website, payments are processed securely by Stripe, Inc. The developer does not receive or store payment details such as credit card numbers.

License key verification is executed entirely offline within the app using Ed25519 cryptographic signatures. No device telemetry, IP logs, or activation calls are transmitted to our servers during verification. The app operates normally in air-gapped or restricted enterprise networks.

4.3. Optional Feedback & Surveys (Google Forms)

If you select "Feedback & bug reports" from the Help menu or Startup Screen, the Application opens a Google Forms page in your system's default web browser.

Participating and providing feedback or contact info is entirely voluntary. Only information you explicitly type into the form is submitted via Google servers to the developer, subject to Google's Privacy Policy. The desktop application does not silently transmit any data.

5. Third-Party Disclosure

Because we do not harvest or retain your project data or personal information, we never sell, share, rent, or disclose your information to any third party.

6. Children's Privacy

Ploto does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13. All capabilities are safe for users of all ages.

7. Changes to This Policy

If this policy is updated, the revised version will be published on this web page with a new "Last updated" date. Material updates may also be noted in app release highlights.
